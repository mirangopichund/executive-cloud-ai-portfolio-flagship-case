
Solution Overview
The proposed solution is a modular, cloud-native platform comprising four integrated layers:

Data & Intelligence Layer

Consolidated customer, network, and usage data lake
Near-real-time analytics for demand, churn, and service risk

Predictive AI Layer

Churn propensity and lifetime value modelling
Demand forecasting to guide fibre expansion
Proactive fault and service degradation prediction

Generative AI Experience Layer

Multilingual virtual assistant for sales, onboarding, and support
AI-driven summarisation and intent classification for contact centres

Decision & Orchestration Layer

Offer personalisation and prioritisation
Next-best-action recommendations
Human-in-the-loop controls for critical decisions


Cloud Architecture Principles
The platform is built on enterprise cloud principles aligned to scale and cost control:

Hybrid cloud deployment to balance agility with regulatory requirements
Event-driven, loosely coupled services for modular evolution
Data minimisation and encryption by design
Built-in observability, cost monitoring, and access controls
This architecture enables selective scaling of AI components without inflating baseline cloud spend.


Responsible AI & Data Governance
Given the consumer-facing nature of fibre services, the solution embeds responsible AI controls by design:

Explicit consent and data usage transparency aligned to POPIA
Bias monitoring in churn and offer models
Explainability for automated recommendations
Human override for retention and credit-impacting decisions
Clear accountability and incident escalation paths
AI is used to augment human decision-making, not replace it.
