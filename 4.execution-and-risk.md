
Execution Roadmap
Phase 1 – Foundation (0–6 months)

Data consolidation and governance setup
Priority use-case selection
Pilot predictive models
Phase 2 – Scale (6–18 months)

GenAI rollout to selected customer journeys
Integration into sales and support processes
Cloud cost and performance optimisation
Phase 3 – Optimisation (18+ months)

Advanced decision orchestration
Continuous model refinement
Expansion of use cases driven by ROI


Key Risks & Mitigations

Model bias or incorrect targeting → ongoing monitoring and human review
Cloud cost overruns → FinOps governance and usage thresholds
Change fatigue → phased rollout and clear ownership
Customer trust erosion → transparency and opt-out controls
