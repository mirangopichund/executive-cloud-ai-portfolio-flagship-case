Strategic Context & Problem Statement
Despite strong demand fundamentals for high-speed connectivity, fibre growth faces persistent challenges:
Slowing penetration in mature coverage areas
High acquisition and service costs eroding margin
Fragmented customer journeys across sales, provisioning, and support
Limited use of predictive insights in network investment decisions
Traditional digital initiatives have improved visibility but not materially changed unit economics or customer outcomes. A step-change is required — one that embeds AI into decision-making rather than layering it onto existing processes.

Strategic Objectives
The platform is designed to deliver against four executive-level objectives:
Grow penetration efficiently by targeting the right customers with the right offers
Reduce cost-to-serve through AI-driven self-service and proactive support
Improve customer experience across the full fibre lifecycle
Optimise capital allocation for fibre rollout and network investment
